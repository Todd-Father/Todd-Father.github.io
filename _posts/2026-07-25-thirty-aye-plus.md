A few people have asked what Thirty-Aye+ actually does, so here's the plain-English version — no assumption you know anything about it already.

## The problem it solves

If you've ever stood on a balcony somewhere along 30A in the morning trying to decide "is today a beach day, a fishing day, or a stay-in day," you already get it. The tide's doing something, the wind's doing something else, and the marine forecast is a wall of numbers nobody wants to decode on vacation.

**Thirty-Aye+** takes real tide, weather, and marine data for the 30A / South Walton coastline and turns it into one simple answer for each of the beach towns along it: what's worth doing right now, and when the good window actually is. No spreadsheet-brain required.

## What you actually see

Pick your town — Seaside, Rosemary Beach, WaterColor, Grayton Beach, Santa Rosa Beach, and the rest of the 11 towns along 30A, or let it use your location — and you land on something like this:

![The Now tab](/assets/images/thirty-aye-plus/phone-mockup.png)

That's the **Now** tab — live temperature, wind, UV, and a one-line "Today's Call" verdict, plus a row of scored activities (beach, surf, fishing, paddle, and a few more below the fold: bike, dolphin watching, hiking) so you can see at a glance what's actually good right now versus what to skip.

Three other tabs round it out:

- **Moments** — instead of a pile of facts, this tab tries to act like a local friend who already knows the town you're in: "the beach is perfect tonight, are we doing happy hour at Shunk Gulley's or dinner and music at Stinky's Fish Camp? If we time it right, ice cream at Blue Mountain Creamery makes a good nightcap." It's meant to start a conversation between the people on the trip, not hand down an order.
- **Scene** — what's happening around your town right now: live music, local flavor, the stuff that changes day to day.
- **Me** — your saved favorites and streak, so it remembers what you actually care about.

## Why it's built town by town

30A isn't one beach — it's a string of small towns, and what makes a good afternoon in Grayton is different from a good afternoon in Rosemary Beach. Thirty-Aye+ is built around that: the town you pick is home base, the plan lives inside it, and the app only points you somewhere else on the rare day it's genuinely worth the drive. That's the whole idea — local specificity a generic weather app can't give you.

## Free app, and the "Plus" experience

There are two layers here, and it's worth being upfront about both.

**Thirty-Aye** (the root site) is the free, no-signup version — real-time tides, weather, and marine conditions scored across seven activities, with local outfitter and rental picks built in. That's intentionally the front door.

**Thirty-Aye+** (`/plus` — the screens above) is the richer, tabbed experience: Now, Moments, Scene, and Me. It's built as the future paid tier, but the honest status today is that the paywall isn't live yet — so right now you can go try the whole thing for free while it's still being built out in the open.

If you're headed to 30A any time soon, pull it up before you decide what to do with your day.
